# Portal Privacy and Support Information — Approval Template

This template requires business/legal approval before publication. It is not legal advice.

## Information Collected

The portal stores account information, client and property contact details, project requirements, communications, approvals, compliance records, procurement records, and uploaded project documents required to administer the construction project.

## Use and Access

Information is used to manage the project and communicate required actions. Access is limited by assigned ADMIN, STAFF, and CLIENT roles. Clients are restricted to their own project information.

## Retention

Name the business owner responsible for the approved retention schedule. The configured document retention period and legal-hold process must match that schedule before normal use.

## Security and Incident Contact

Publish a monitored email address and telephone escalation route for access problems, suspected account misuse, incorrect client visibility, or requests concerning personal information.

## User Responsibilities

Users must protect their credentials, use unique passwords, sign out on shared devices, avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive information, and report unexpected access immediately.
